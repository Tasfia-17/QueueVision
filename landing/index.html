The Problem

Mobile moderation is broken

Over 60% of Reddit moderators use mobile as their primary device, yet the native modqueue gives them almost no context to make good decisions. The result: slow responses, wrong calls, and mod burnout.

🕳️

Zero context

The default queue shows a title and a report reason. No thread history, no user background, no prior mod actions. Mods are flying blind.

💥

Mod collisions

Two mods act on the same item simultaneously. One approves, one removes. No coordination layer means wasted effort and contradictory outcomes.

🐢

Slow triage

Every decision requires opening the post, checking the user profile, reviewing mod notes - in separate tabs. A 30-second decision takes 5 minutes.

📵

No prioritization

Urgent spam sits next to low-risk off-topic posts with no visual distinction. High-impact items get buried under noise.

The Solution

Everything a mod needs, in one tap

QueueVision is a Devvit app that replaces the modqueue experience with a full-context decision cockpit - built mobile-first, zero configuration required.

🧵

ThreadRecon

See the full conversation: parent chain, reported comment highlighted, sibling context, thread stats, and report density - all in one panel.

👤

UserRadar

Instant user intelligence: account age, community karma, prior mod actions, mod notes, and risk flags like new account or prior ban.

⚡

ActionPalette

One-tap macros for Approve, Clean Remove, Spam Ban, Mute, Warn & Release. High-risk actions get a 3-second undo window.

🔒

ClaimLock

Claim an item before acting. Realtime pub/sub shows teammates what's being handled, eliminating duplicate work and contradictory decisions.

📊

SmartQueue

Risk scoring algorithm buckets items into Urgent / Standard / Batch based on report count, account age, karma, and AutoMod flags.

📋

ModTrail

Full audit log of every action taken on an item. Accountability for the team, context for the next mod who picks it up.

How it works

From report to resolution in seconds

1

Item enters the queue

A post or comment is reported or flagged by AutoMod. QueueVision's event triggers capture it instantly and compute a risk score based on report count, account signals, and content flags.

2

Mod opens QueueVision

Tap "Investigate with QueueVision" from any post or comment menu. The app opens in a full-screen webview with the item pre-loaded - no navigation required.

3

Full context loads instantly

ThreadRecon shows the conversation. UserRadar shows the author's history. ModTrail shows prior actions. All fetched in parallel from Reddit API with Redis caching for speed.

4

Claim and act

Claim the item to signal your team. Then tap a macro - Approve, Remove, Ban, or Warn. High-risk actions show a 3-second undo banner before executing.

5

Audit trail recorded

Every action is logged to ModTrail with timestamp and moderator identity. The next mod who opens the item sees exactly what happened and why.
